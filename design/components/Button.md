# Button

One primary button per screen; everything else steps down.

- **Primary** (`accent`, `on-accent`, `radius-lg`, 16px semibold, `shadow-accent`): the one thing this screen is for. Full width, at the bottom of the content or the sheet. An icon is allowed when it adds meaning (pot for cooking, basket for groceries).
- **Secondary** (`surface`, `ink`, `shadow-card`): the alternative, directly under the primary ("Voir la recette").
- **About P** (`iris-strong`): actions aimed at the partner ("Relancer P").
- **Compact in-card** (`sage-strong`, 14px bold, 12px radius): an action that belongs to a tinted card, on the card's title line, so card and action read as one ("Ajouter" on the Courses card). Never a full-width terracotta bar inside a tinted card.
- **Disabled**: `line` fill, `faint` text, no shadow, plus one short line above saying what is missing.
- **Link**: `accent-text`, 15px medium, for tertiary actions ("Finalement, pas ce soir").
