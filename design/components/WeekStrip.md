# WeekStrip

Seven days under the Ce soir title; tapping opens This week.

- Today: `accent` circle, `on-accent` number, its letter in `accent-text`.
- Evenings you eat together: `accent-soft` circle. Other evenings: no circle, `faint` number.
- Marks on the circle: a small `sage` check when the meal was cooked; a tiny photo dot when a recipe is already chosen.
- No other information in the strip.
