# Catalogue fit (November 2026)

How our own recipes (the `original` collection, the ones the app shows) do against the six
[weeknight rules](recipe-library-plan.md#weeknight-rules): healthy, 40 min or less, high protein
(30 g a serving and 25 % of the calories), everyday ingredients, a short list (10 lines or fewer that
aren't staples or optional garnishes) and the weeknight shape. Numbers come from the app's own
classifier (`classify.ts`, `isHealthy`) on `supabase/seed/recipes.json`, per serving.

Nothing is removed for failing: the deck learns from taste tests which of these a household wants.
